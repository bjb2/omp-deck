# Business and Content Operations

## Workflow
1. Clarify audience, decision, channel, evidence standard, and desired action.
2. Reuse supplied company facts, brand voice, and existing assets.
3. Research current external claims when material; cite sources.
4. Produce channel-native output rather than identical cross-posts.
5. Keep financial/legal claims factual, internally consistent, and appropriately caveated.
6. End with concrete decisions, owners, metrics, or next actions.

## Quality
Avoid generic AI prose, invented traction, fake quotes, unsupported market sizes, and manipulative outreach. Preserve consent and platform rules.

## Archived references
Investor, marketing, sales, logistics, compliance, and industry-specialist skills remain archived and should be opened only for matching work.
